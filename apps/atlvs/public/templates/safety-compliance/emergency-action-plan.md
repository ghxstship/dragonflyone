## Emergency Procedures

### 1. FIRE

**Immediate Actions:**
1. **ALERT** - Pull fire alarm / Call 911
2. **EVACUATE** - Begin evacuation procedures
3. **CONTAIN** - Close doors to contain fire (if safe)
4. **EXTINGUISH** - Use fire extinguisher only if:
   - Fire is small
   - You are trained
   - You have clear exit path

**Radio Code:** "CODE RED - [Location]"

**Evacuation Announcement:**
> "Attention please. We are experiencing an emergency situation. Please calmly proceed to the nearest exit. Follow the directions of staff members. Do not use elevators. Proceed to the assembly area in the [location]."

### 2. MEDICAL EMERGENCY

**Immediate Actions:**
1. Call for medical team on CH 7
2. Clear area around patient
3. Do not move patient unless in immediate danger
4. Stay with patient until medical arrives
5. Provide information to medical team

**Radio Code:** "CODE BLUE - [Location]"

**Information to Provide:**
- Location
- Nature of emergency
- Number of patients
- Patient condition (conscious/breathing)
- Any known cause

### 3. SEVERE WEATHER

**Weather Monitoring:**
- Monitor weather radar every 30 minutes
- Lightning detection system active
- Wind speed monitoring

**Action Levels:**

| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Watch issued | Monitor closely, prepare for action |
| Warning issued | Prepare for shelter/evacuation |
| Lightning <10 miles | Suspend outdoor activities |
| Tornado warning | Immediate shelter |
| High winds >40 mph | Evaluate structures |

**Radio Code:** "CODE YELLOW - [Weather Type]"

**Shelter Locations:**

**Shelter Announcement:**

### 4. ACTIVE THREAT / VIOLENCE

**Immediate Actions - RUN, HIDE, FIGHT:**

**RUN** (if safe path exists):
- Leave belongings
- Help others if possible
- Prevent others from entering
- Call 911 when safe

**HIDE** (if escape not possible):
- Lock/barricade doors
- Turn off lights
- Silence phones
- Hide behind solid objects
- Remain quiet

**FIGHT** (last resort only):
- Commit to action
- Use improvised weapons
- Act with aggression
- Incapacitate threat

**Radio Code:** "CODE BLACK - [Location]"

**DO NOT:**
- Approach the threat
- Attempt to negotiate
- Pull fire alarm (may cause crowd to enter danger)

---

### 5. BOMB THREAT

**If Threat Received by Phone:**
1. Stay calm, keep caller talking
2. Note exact words used
3. Ask: Where? When? What kind? Why?
4. Note caller details (voice, background noise)
5. Do not hang up
6. Signal colleague to call 911

**Radio Code:** "CODE ORANGE"

**Search Procedure:**
- Do not use radios near suspicious items
- Do not touch suspicious items
- Clear area 300+ feet
- Wait for authorities

**Evacuation Decision:**
- Made by Incident Commander in consultation with police
- Consider: credibility, specificity, venue layout

---

### 6. CROWD EMERGENCY

**Warning Signs:**
- Crowd surge or crush
- People falling
- Difficulty breathing
- Panic or screaming
- Crowd density too high

**Immediate Actions:**
1. Notify Production immediately
2. Stop show if necessary
3. Open additional exits/barriers
4. Deploy crowd management team
5. Create space/relief areas
6. Provide medical assistance

**Radio Code:** "CODE PURPLE - [Location]"

**Show Stop Procedure:**
1. Stage Manager calls "SHOW STOP"
2. Music stops immediately
3. House lights to full
4. Artist makes calming announcement
5. Security creates relief areas
6. Medical teams deploy

---

### 7. UTILITY FAILURE

**Power Failure:**
1. Emergency lighting activates automatically
2. Assess situation - partial or complete
3. Notify venue management
4. Consider evacuation if extended
5. Secure cash and valuables

**Radio Code:** "CODE GRAY - POWER"

**Water/Plumbing:**
1. Shut off water if flooding
2. Notify venue management
3. Clean up hazards
4. Consider restroom alternatives

**Gas Leak:**
1. Do not use electrical switches
2. Evacuate immediately
3. Call 911 and gas company
4. Do not re-enter until cleared

## Post-Incident Procedures

1. **Secure Scene** - Preserve evidence if needed
2. **Account for Personnel** - Verify all staff safe
3. **Document** - Complete incident reports
4. **Notify** - Insurance, legal, management
5. **Debrief** - Conduct post-incident review
6. **Support** - Provide resources for affected persons
7. **Review** - Update plans based on lessons learned
