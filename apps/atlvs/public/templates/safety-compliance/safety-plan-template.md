**Evacuation Procedure:**
1. Safety Director initiates evacuation via radio
2. PA announcement: "[Scripted announcement]"
3. House lights to full
4. Music/show stops immediately
5. Security directs crowd to exits
6. Staff sweep all areas
7. Account for all personnel at assembly point
8. Do not re-enter until all-clear given

### 2.2 Medical Emergency
1. Call for medical team on CH 7
2. Clear area around patient
3. Do not move patient unless immediate danger
4. Medical team assesses and treats
5. Call 911 if needed
6. Document incident
7. Notify Safety Director

### 2.3 Fire Emergency
1. Activate fire alarm
2. Call 911
3. Notify Safety Director on CH 1
4. Begin evacuation
5. Attempt to extinguish only if safe and trained
6. Meet fire department at designated location
7. Do not re-enter until cleared by fire department

### 2.5 Security Threat
1. Do not confront
2. Notify Security Director on CH 5
3. Provide description and location
4. Follow Security Director's instructions
5. Evacuate if directed
6. Cooperate with law enforcement

### 2.6 Crowd Management Emergency
**Signs of Crowd Distress:**
- Crowd surge or crush
- People falling
- Difficulty breathing
- Panic or screaming

**Response:**
1. Notify Production and Security immediately
2. Stop show if necessary
3. Open additional exits/barriers
4. Deploy crowd management team
5. Provide medical assistance
6. Consider partial or full evacuation
