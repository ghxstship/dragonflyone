## 1. Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any and all information disclosed by the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party, whether orally, in writing, or by any other means, including but not limited to:

- [ ] Business plans and strategies
- [ ] Financial information and projections
- [ ] Customer and vendor lists
- [ ] Marketing plans and materials
- [ ] Technical data and specifications
- [ ] Trade secrets and know-how
- [ ] Creative content and concepts
- [ ] Production schedules and logistics
- [ ] Personnel information
- [ ] Contracts and agreements
- [ ] Software and technology
- [ ] Any other proprietary information

Confidential Information includes information that is:
- Marked as "Confidential" or similar designation
- Identified orally as confidential at the time of disclosure
- Reasonably understood to be confidential given its nature

---

## 2. Exclusions from Confidential Information

Confidential Information does not include information that:

a) Is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party;

b) Was in the Receiving Party's possession prior to disclosure, as evidenced by written records;

c) Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of Confidential Information;

d) Is rightfully obtained from a third party without restriction on disclosure;

e) Is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or court order, provided the Receiving Party gives prompt notice to the Disclosing Party.

---

## 3. Obligations of Receiving Party

The Receiving Party agrees to:

a) **Maintain Confidentiality:** Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent of the Disclosing Party;

b) **Limit Use:** Use Confidential Information solely for the purpose stated in this Agreement;

c) **Limit Access:** Restrict access to Confidential Information to those employees, agents, or contractors who have a need to know and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as restrictive as this Agreement;

d) **Protect Information:** Use the same degree of care to protect Confidential Information as it uses to protect its own confidential information, but in no event less than reasonable care;

e) **No Copies:** Not copy or reproduce Confidential Information except as necessary for the permitted purpose;

f) **Return or Destroy:** Upon request or termination of this Agreement, promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information and certify such return or destruction in writing.
