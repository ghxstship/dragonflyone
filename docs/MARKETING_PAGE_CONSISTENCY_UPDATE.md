## Brand Voice Guidelines (Updated)

### Tone
- **Authoritative** — We're the leader, not a challenger
- **Direct** — No hedging, no "might" or "could"
- **Confident** — State facts, not aspirations
- **Practical** — Focus on outcomes, not features

### Do Say
- "The industry standard for live entertainment"
- "Built for productions, activations, installations, and destinations"
- "Modular by design. Use what you need."
- "Compatible with your existing tools"
- "Scales from your first event to your hundredth"

### Don't Say
- "We're disrupting the industry" (we ARE the industry)
- "Cutting-edge" or "revolutionary" (overused, meaningless)
- "Best-in-class" (everyone says this)
- "We're different" (show, don't tell)

### Headline Formulas
| Formula | Example |
|---------|---------|
| `THE [NOUN] FOR [VERTICAL]` | THE PLATFORM FOR LIVE ENTERTAINMENT |
| `[MODULAR]. [COMPATIBLE]. [SCALABLE].` | Modular. Compatible. Scalable. |
| `[ACTION] ANY [VERTICAL]` | POWER ANY PRODUCTION |
