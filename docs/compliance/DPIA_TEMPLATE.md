## 1. DPIA Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

This Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) template is designed to help GHXSTSHIP Industries identify and minimize data protection risks associated with new projects, systems, or processing activities. DPIAs are required under GDPR Article 35 when processing is likely to result in high risk to individuals' rights and freedoms.

### 1.2 When is a DPIA Required?

A DPIA is **mandatory** when:

- [ ] Systematic and extensive profiling with significant effects
- [ ] Large-scale processing of special category data
- [ ] Systematic monitoring of publicly accessible areas
- [ ] Using new technologies that may pose high risks
- [ ] Processing that prevents individuals from exercising rights
- [ ] Automated decision-making with legal or significant effects
- [ ] Large-scale processing of children's data
- [ ] Processing that could result in physical harm if breached

A DPIA is **recommended** when:

- [ ] Combining datasets from different sources
- [ ] Processing vulnerable individuals' data
- [ ] Innovative use of existing technologies
- [ ] Processing that involves tracking individuals
- [ ] Processing biometric or genetic data
