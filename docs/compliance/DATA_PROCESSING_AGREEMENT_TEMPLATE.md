## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1** "Agreement" means the main service agreement between the Parties.

**1.2** "Data Protection Laws" means all applicable laws relating to data protection, including GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA, LGPD, and any other applicable data protection legislation.

**1.3** "GDPR" means the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679.

**1.4** "Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person processed under this DPA.

**1.5** "Processing" means any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, storage, use, disclosure, or deletion.

**1.6** "Sub-processor" means any third party engaged by the Processor to process Personal Data.

**1.7** "Data Subject" means the individual to whom Personal Data relates.

**1.8** "Supervisory Authority" means an independent public authority responsible for monitoring data protection compliance.

**2.1** This DPA applies to the Processing of Personal Data by the Processor on behalf of the Controller in connection with the Agreement.

**2.2** The subject matter, duration, nature, and purpose of Processing, as well as the types of Personal Data and categories of Data Subjects, are described in **Schedule 1**.

## 3. PROCESSOR OBLIGATIONS

**3.1 Instructions**
The Processor shall:
- (a) Process Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Controller, unless required by law;
- (b) Inform the Controller if an instruction infringes Data Protection Laws;
- (c) Immediately notify the Controller if it believes an instruction violates applicable law.

**3.2 Confidentiality**
The Processor shall:
- (a) Ensure that persons authorized to process Personal Data are bound by confidentiality obligations;
- (b) Not disclose Personal Data to any third party except as permitted by this DPA.

**3.3 Security**
The Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including:
- (a) Encryption of Personal Data at rest and in transit;
- (b) Measures to ensure ongoing confidentiality, integrity, availability, and resilience;
- (c) Regular testing and evaluation of security measures;
- (d) Measures described in **Schedule 2**.

**3.4 Sub-processing**
The Processor shall:
- (a) Not engage a Sub-processor without prior written authorization from the Controller;
- (b) Maintain an up-to-date list of Sub-processors (see **Schedule 3**);
- (c) Impose data protection obligations on Sub-processors equivalent to this DPA;
- (d) Remain liable for Sub-processor compliance.

**3.5 Data Subject Rights**
The Processor shall:
- (a) Assist the Controller in responding to Data Subject requests;
- (b) Notify the Controller promptly of any Data Subject request received;
- (c) Not respond directly to Data Subject requests without Controller authorization.

**3.6 Data Protection Impact Assessments**
The Processor shall assist the Controller with DPIAs and prior consultations with Supervisory Authorities where required.

**3.7 Personal Data Breach**
The Processor shall:
- (a) Notify the Controller without undue delay (within 24 hours) upon becoming aware of a Personal Data breach;
- (b) Provide sufficient information to enable the Controller to meet breach notification obligations;
- (c) Cooperate with the Controller in investigating and remediating the breach.

**3.8 Deletion and Return**
Upon termination of the Agreement, the Processor shall:
- (a) Delete or return all Personal Data to the Controller, at the Controller's choice;
- (b) Delete existing copies unless retention is required by law;
- (c) Provide certification of deletion upon request.

**3.9 Audits**
The Processor shall:
- (a) Make available information necessary to demonstrate compliance;
- (b) Allow for and contribute to audits conducted by the Controller or an auditor;
- (c) Provide audit reports upon reasonable request.

---

## 4. CONTROLLER OBLIGATIONS

**4.1** The Controller shall:
- (a) Ensure it has a lawful basis for Processing Personal Data;
- (b) Provide documented instructions to the Processor;
- (c) Ensure Data Subjects are informed of the Processing;
- (d) Respond to Data Subject requests in accordance with Data Protection Laws.

---

## 5. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

**5.1** The Processor shall not transfer Personal Data outside the EEA/UK unless:
- (a) The transfer is to a country with an adequacy decision;
- (b) Appropriate safeguards are in place (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses);
- (c) A derogation applies under Data Protection Laws.

**5.2** Where Standard Contractual Clauses are required, the Parties agree to execute the EU Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses as set out in **Schedule 4**.

**5.3** The Processor shall implement supplementary measures where necessary to ensure adequate protection.

---

## 6. LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

**6.1** Each Party shall be liable for damages caused by Processing that infringes Data Protection Laws.

**6.2** The Processor shall indemnify the Controller for any fines, penalties, or damages arising from the Processor's breach of this DPA.

**6.3** Liability limitations in the Agreement shall apply to this DPA, except for:
- (a) Breaches of confidentiality obligations;
- (b) Willful misconduct or gross negligence;
- (c) Regulatory fines.

---

## 7. TERM AND TERMINATION

**7.1** This DPA shall remain in effect for the duration of the Agreement.

**7.2** Obligations relating to confidentiality and data deletion shall survive termination.

**7.3** Either Party may terminate this DPA upon material breach by the other Party that remains uncured for 30 days after written notice.

---

## 8. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**8.1 Amendments**
This DPA may only be amended in writing signed by both Parties.

**8.2 Governing Law**
This DPA shall be governed by the laws of [Jurisdiction].

**8.3 Dispute Resolution**
Disputes shall be resolved in accordance with the dispute resolution provisions of the Agreement.

**8.4 Entire Agreement**
This DPA, together with its Schedules, constitutes the entire agreement regarding data processing.

**8.5 Severability**
If any provision is found invalid, the remaining provisions shall continue in effect.
