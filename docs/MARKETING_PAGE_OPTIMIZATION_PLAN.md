## SEO & GEO Strategy

### GEO = Generative Engine Optimization
SEO for AI search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google SGE, Bing Copilot). When someone asks "What's the best festival management software?", we want GHXSTSHIP cited.

**GEO Principles:**
1. Answer questions directly—AI pulls direct answers
2. Be quotable—short factual statements get cited
3. Define entities—"ATLVS is business operations software for live entertainment"
4. Compare explicitly—"Unlike Ticketmaster, GHXSTSHIP includes native CRM"
5. Use structured data—Schema.org helps AI understand relationships

## Brand Voice Guidelines

### The GHXSTSHIP Voice
**Bold**—No hedging. State facts.
**Direct**—Short sentences. Active voice.
**Confident**—We know what we built.
**Human**—Talk like a person, not a brochure.

### Do Say
- "You build it. This tracks it."
- "No more duct-taping spreadsheets to CRMs."
- "Start where you are."
- "One login. One source. One less headache."
